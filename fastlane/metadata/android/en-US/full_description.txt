Kapow reads the comics you already own — CBZ, CBR (RAR4 and RAR5) and PDF — on any Android phone or tablet. On a foldable it adapts to the posture: folded, a clean one-page reader; unfolded, the double-page spread as printed; on the table, the page stays up.

READ YOUR WAY
• Guided View: panel by panel, detected on your device — nothing leaves your phone.
• Bigger speech bubbles: enlarge the lettering so small print stops being a problem.
• Edge taps or volume keys turn the page, pinch to zoom, left-to-right or right-to-left reading.
• Night tint and keep-screen-on while reading.

YOUR SHELF
• Point Kapow at your comics folder; it builds the bookshelf, groups issues by series and keeps it up to date.
• Continue reading, unread / read / favourites filters, search and sort.
• Per-comic settings that remember how you like to read each title.

YOUR LOOK
• Pure black (OLED), graphite or paper themes and seven accent colours, or the wallpaper colour on Android 12+.
• English and Spanish.

PRIVACY
Kapow collects no data and has no accounts, ads or trackers. It only reads the files you point it to. Guided View and enlarged bubbles run entirely on the device; they work best on comics with clearly drawn panels and may miss on some pages — both are still being improved.
