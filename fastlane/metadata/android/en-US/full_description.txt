Kapow reads the comics you already own — CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT and PDF — on any Android phone or tablet. On a foldable it adapts to the posture: folded, a clean one-page reader; unfolded, the double-page spread as printed; on the table, the page stays up.

READ YOUR WAY
• Guided View: panel by panel, detected on your device — nothing leaves your phone.
• Bigger speech bubbles: the lettering grows so small print stops being a problem.
• Comic, Manga (right to left) or Webcomic (vertical scroll), chosen per comic.
• Page look for tired scans — Original, Brighter, More contrast or Paper — and fit to screen or to width.
• Edge taps or volume keys turn the page, pinch to zoom, bookmark a page, share the page you are on.
• Night tint and keep-screen-on while reading.

YOUR SHELF
• Point Kapow at your comics folder; it builds the shelf, groups issues by series and keeps it up to date even when you rename or move a file.
• Reading lists you order yourself, and "Next issue" follows them.
• Search by field — writer:, series:, year>2015, rating:4+ — or tap a preset: In progress, Highly rated, Recently added.
• ComicInfo.xml metadata: the real series, issue, year, story title and credits. Rate a comic, correct its details, make any page its cover.
• Reading stats: pages, time and pace over the last 30 days, measured on this device only.

YOUR LOOK
• Pure black (OLED), graphite or paper themes, seven accent colours or the wallpaper colour on Android 12+, and five launcher icons.
• English and Spanish.

PRIVACY
Kapow collects no data and has no accounts, ads or trackers. It only reads the files you point it to. Guided View and enlarged bubbles run entirely on the device; they work best on comics with clearly drawn panels and may miss on some pages — both are still being improved.
